# Idea

**Command:** `/dumdum:idea`

## What It Does

Tell Claude your idea, no matter how rough or half-formed, and it will help you shape it into something clear and buildable. Through a short, friendly conversation, Claude asks you a few questions, one at a time, to understand what you want to create. You do not need to know any technical terms or have everything figured out.

## When to Use It

Use this when you have an idea for something you want to build but have not written it down or organized it yet. This is a great starting point if you are not sure where to begin.

## Example Conversation

> **You:** I want to make a website where people can find local musicians for hire.
>
> **Claude:** That sounds fun. Who would be hiring these musicians? People planning parties, event venues, something else?
>
> **You:** Mostly people planning weddings or birthday parties.
>
> **Claude:** Got it. And when someone finds a musician they like, what should they be able to do? Send a message, book them directly, see their prices?
>
> **You:** I think just send a message to start. Maybe booking later.

## What You Get at the End

An **idea summary** that includes:

- A short, memorable name for your project
- A plain English description of what it does
- Who it is for
- The most important features (up to 5)
- What the project will NOT try to do, so the scope stays focused

## Suggested Next Skills

- `/dumdum:who-is-it-for` to think more about the people who will use this and what they need
- `/dumdum:break-it-down` to split your idea into small, buildable pieces
- `/dumdum:pick-tools` to figure out what technology to use
