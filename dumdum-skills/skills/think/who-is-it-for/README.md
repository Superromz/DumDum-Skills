# Who Is It For

**Command:** `/dumdum:who-is-it-for`

## What It Does

This skill helps you figure out who will actually use your project and what they need from it. Claude walks you through identifying the different types of people who might use what you are building, then explores what each type of person wants, what would frustrate them, and what would make them happy.

## When to Use It

Use this after you have a rough idea of what you want to build. Understanding your users early on makes every decision easier, from what features to build first to how things should look.

## Example Conversation

> **You:** I am building an app that helps people split restaurant bills.
>
> **Claude:** Nice. Who do you picture using this? Friends at dinner, coworkers at lunch, families?
>
> **You:** Mostly groups of friends eating out together.
>
> **Claude:** When a group of friends uses your app, what is the first thing they would want to do? Pick the person who is going to divide everything up, or does everyone open the app on their own phone?
>
> **You:** I think one person opens it, enters the items, and then shows everyone what they owe.

## What You Get at the End

A **user guide** that describes each type of user, including:

- Who they are, described as real people (not statistics)
- What they need to accomplish
- What would make them happy
- What would make them give up and leave
