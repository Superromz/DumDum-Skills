# Make It Look Good

**Command:** `/dumdum:make-it-look-good`

## What it does

Improves the visual design of your project so it looks polished and professional. It adjusts colors, spacing, fonts, and layout, then explains every design choice in plain English so you can tweak things later if you want.

## When to use it

Your project works but looks rough, unfinished, or hard to read. You want it to feel like something you would be proud to show someone.

## Example conversation

> **You:** /dumdum:make-it-look-good
>
> **Claude:** I will look at your project and suggest visual improvements. Any preferences on style? For example: clean and minimal, bold and colorful, dark theme.
>
> **You:** Clean and minimal. I like lots of white space.
>
> **Claude:** Great. I am updating the colors, spacing, and typography to give it a clean, airy feel. Here is what I changed and why each choice works.

## What you get at the end

Updated visual design with better colors, spacing, and layout. Every change is explained so you know how to adjust things yourself.

## Suggested next skills

- `/dumdum:test-it` to make sure the design looks right across different screens
- `/dumdum:add-feature` to keep building on your improved project
