# What's Left

**Command:** `/dumdum:whats-left`

## What it does

Looks at your project and builds a simple checklist of remaining work. It organizes tasks by priority so you know what to tackle next, what can wait, and what is already done.

## When to use it

You want to know how much work is left, or you need help deciding what to do next.

## Example conversation

> **You:** /dumdum:whats-left
>
> **Claude:** Here is your project checklist. Two items are done, three are next up, and two can wait. Want me to help you start on the top priority?
>
> **You:** What is the top priority?
>
> **Claude:** Adding form validation to the signup page. Right now it accepts empty submissions, which will cause problems.

## What you get at the end

A prioritized checklist showing what is done, what is next, and what can wait.

## Suggested next skills
