# Take Notes

**Command:** `/dumdum:take-notes`

## What it does

Saves a note about a decision, idea, or reminder into a project journal. Each entry is timestamped so you can look back and remember why you made certain choices or what you were planning to do.

## When to use it

You want to record a thought before you forget it, document why you made a decision, or leave yourself a reminder for next time.

## Example conversation

> **You:** /dumdum:take-notes
>
> **Claude:** What do you want to write down? It can be a decision, an idea, a reminder, or anything else.
>
> **You:** I decided to use a simple list instead of a database because this project is small.
>
> **Claude:** Got it. I saved that to your project journal with today's date. You can review all your notes anytime.

## What you get at the end

A timestamped journal entry saved to your project.

## Suggested next skills

- `/dumdum:whats-left` to see your remaining work alongside your notes
- `/dumdum:save-progress` to save a checkpoint of your current work
