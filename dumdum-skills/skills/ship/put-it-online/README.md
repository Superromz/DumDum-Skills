# Put It Online

**Command:** `/dumdum:put-it-online`

## What it does

Walks you through putting your project on the internet, step by step. It picks the simplest hosting option for your project type, handles the setup, and gives you a shareable link when it is done.

## When to use it

Your project works locally and you want other people to be able to see it or use it on the web.

## Example conversation

> **You:** /dumdum:put-it-online
>
> **Claude:** Let me look at your project to figure out the best way to host it. It looks like a static website, so I will use a free hosting service. Ready to start?
>
> **You:** Yes, let's do it.
>
> **Claude:** Done. Your project is live at this URL. Here is how to update it when you make changes.

## What you get at the end

Your project live on the internet with a shareable URL.
