# Save Progress

**Command:** `/dumdum:save-progress`

## What it does

Saves a checkpoint of your work with a clear description of what changed. It creates a snapshot you can return to later, and shows you a history of your past saves so you always know where you have been.

## When to use it

You finished a chunk of work and want to lock it in before moving on, or you want to see what you have saved so far.

## Example conversation

> **You:** /dumdum:save-progress
>
> **Claude:** What did you just finish working on? Describe it in a sentence or two.
>
> **You:** I added a contact form to the homepage.
>
> **Claude:** Got it. I saved a checkpoint called "Added contact form to homepage." Here are your last few saves so you can see the history.

## What you get at the end

A saved checkpoint you can return to, plus a history of past saves.
